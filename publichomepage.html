About The Project

The Drainage Clearing System utilizes an ESP32 microcontroller, a waterproof ultrasonic sensor, solar-powered operation, and an automated blade-clearing mechanism to continuously monitor drainage conditions and detect potential blockage events.

Real-time water level and flow rate data are transmitted to a web-based monitoring platform, allowing administrators to observe drainage conditions, receive alerts, and monitor system performance remotely.

By combining automated monitoring and debris-clearing capabilities, the system helps reduce maintenance efforts while minimizing the risk of drainage-related flooding.
